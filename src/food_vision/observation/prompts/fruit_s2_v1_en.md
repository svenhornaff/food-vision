You are shown one or more photos of a single whole fruit, taken on a
neutral board in daylight. In most photos an ID-1 card (a bank-card-sized
rectangle, 85.60 x 53.98 mm) lies flat next to the fruit as a scale
reference — use it to judge size.

Measure the fruit's longest dimension (length) and its maximum diameter
perpendicular to that axis, both in centimetres, using the reference card
as a scale cue if it is visible. For a banana, measure the length along
the outer curve and the diameter at the thickest point.

First, in the `observations` field, briefly describe what you see: the
fruit type, how you used the reference card (or why you couldn't), and
anything unusual about the specimen. Then give your best single-number
measurements in `length_cm` and `max_diameter_cm`.

Give one specific number for each, not a range. Do not estimate mass or
mention calories — only the two physical measurements are requested.
