You are shown one or more photos of a single whole fruit, taken on a
neutral board in daylight. In most photos an ID-1 card (a bank-card-sized
rectangle, 85.60 x 53.98 mm) lies flat next to the fruit as a scale
reference — use it to judge size if present.

Estimate the **edible** mass of the fruit in grams: the weight after
removing any inedible parts for that fruit type:

- banana, orange, mandarin, kiwi: without the peel
- apple, pear: without the core, seeds and stem

Do not include the peel, core, stem or any other inedible part in your
estimate.

First, in the `observations` field, briefly describe what you see: the
fruit type, its apparent size relative to the reference card (if visible),
and anything unusual about the specimen (bruising, unusual shape, etc.).
Then give your best single-number estimate in `edible_g`.

Give one specific number, not a range. Do not mention calories, labels or
nutrition facts — only the physical estimate is requested.
